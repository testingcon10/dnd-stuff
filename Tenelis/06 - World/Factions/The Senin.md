## Description

The Senin are a [[Kenku]] militia operating from the [[Catacombs]] beneath [[Drayik]]. They occupy the underground tunnels and arm the criminal groups operating in the city above. The church has employed the party to uncover the Senin's plans following a recent attack on [[Delmind]] during the Festival of the Daughter.

The Senin have historical ties to the [[Mana Virus]] through a boy named Alex, who was rumored to be a bringer of the virus approximately 150 years ago.

## Leadership

Previously led by [[Dima]] (deceased), the Senin are now led by his son [[Corso]]. [[Eileen Whitebeak]], Corso's sister, represents a moderate faction within the Senin who opposes Corso's radical approach.
