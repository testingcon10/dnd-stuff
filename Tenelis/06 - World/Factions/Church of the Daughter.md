## Description

The Church of the Daughter is a religious order that controls [[Old Drayik Keep]] in [[Drayik]]. Founded approximately 150 years ago by Father Ellen after he lost his daughter to the [[Mana Virus]], the church grew from his legacy as a prophet and miracle worker who could cure the afflicted.

The church has employed the party to investigate the Senin following a recent attack on [[Delmind]] during the Festival of the Daughter.

## Goals

- Uncover the Senin's plans

## Operations

- Sends men into the [[Catacombs]] beneath [[Drayik]] - none have returned
- Employed the party to investigate the Senin after the attack on [[Delmind]]
- Gave tacit approval to the party's disruption of the [[Drayik]] criminal gangs, on the condition nothing ties back to the church
- Keeps a library and records room in [[Old Drayik Keep]] - the records room is highly confidential

## History

- Founded approximately 150 years ago by Father Ellen
- [[Old Drayik Keep]] was used as ground zero during the [[Mana Virus]] - a major hospital for treating the afflicted. Those who spent enough time with the virus were sealed in the keep, in the [[Catacombs]]
- The keep was formerly home to the [[Knights of Drayik]]
