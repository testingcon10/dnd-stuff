# Festival of the Daughter

## Overview

A festival associated with the [[Church of the Daughter]]. A recent attack on [[Delmind]] occurred during the Festival of the Daughter, which prompted the church to employ the party to investigate [[The Senin|the Senin]].

## Session Discoveries

### Session 5

- After the attack on [[Delmind]] during the Festival of the Daughter, the church employed the party to [[Uncover the Senin|uncover the Senin]]'s plans
- [[Eileen Whitebeak]] referenced the attack - after the attack on [[Delmind]], she realized violence will not change the opinion of her people
