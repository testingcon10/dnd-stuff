# Knights of [[Drayik]]

## Overview

## Session Discoveries

### Session 6

- [[Avo Red]] was the founding knight of the Knights of [[Drayik]]. A feudalist leader ruled [[Drayik]] cruelly, oppressing the lower class. [[Avo Red]] stood up for the people and conquered the city. However, he left very quickly after - as if he was on his own mission

### Session 11

- The Knights of [[Drayik]] were one of the leading forces that helped secure victory during the great demon war (~200 years ago), led by the forces of the archdemon Grazid. Many knights died in the conflict. It was believed all demons were vanquished or fled afterward - but a surviving demon is now thought to be living in the mountains near [[Little Hill]]
