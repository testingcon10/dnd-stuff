# Underground Networks

## Overview

## Session Discoveries

### Session 5

- [[Kenku]] occupy the catacombs beneath [[Drayik]] and are arming all the criminal groups in the city

### Session 6

- [[Kenku]] military book (found by [[Cassius Bellona|Cassius]]): [[Kenku]] are afraid of pushing into mountainous terrain. Rivers believed to lead to an underground river network flowing beneath [[Drayik]]. Multiple church expeditions theorize the [[Tri Rivers]] flow underground beneath the city - but insufficient data due to lack of expeditions
- Getting into [[Old Drayik Keep]] has been impossible - may be magic, a curse, or an enchanted door. Immune to spells. Theory that it's an entrance to the [[Catacombs]]
- [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] found an extremely thorough report (~98% complete survey/census of [[Drayik]]) documenting basements, tunnels, and infrastructure. Discovered coastal caves that may be a lead to the [[Catacombs]]
- Cave system exists outside of [[Drayik]] - rough estimate of location but no pinpointed spot
- [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] took notes of all basements with underground connections
- An underground river tributary flows beneath the shopping district - [[Red Elk]]? [[Boar Tusk Brewery]]? Needs exploration
- [[Drayik]] had to quarantine during the [[Mana Virus]]. [[Old Drayik Keep]] was used as ground zero - a major hospital for treating the afflicted. Those who spent enough time with the virus had to be sealed away. The afflicted were sealed shut in the keep, in the [[Catacombs]]
