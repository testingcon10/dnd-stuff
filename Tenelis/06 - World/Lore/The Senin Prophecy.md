# [[The Senin]] Prophecy

## Overview

An ancient prophecy tied to [[The Senin|the Senin]] people. The prophecy previously led to the genocide of [[The Senin|the Senin]], and [[Corso]] - current leader of the [[The Senin|Senin militia]] - is now obsessed with it. His sister [[Eileen Whitebeak]] refuses to follow this path, believing it will lead to more death.

## What Is Known

- [[Corso]] is obsessed with the prophecy
- The prophecy led to the genocide of [[The Senin|the Senin]] people in the past
- [[Eileen Whitebeak]] refuses to follow it - she chose the prosperity of her people over the prophecy path
- The full meaning of the prophecy remains unknown to the party
- Active quest objective: find out what the prophecy means

## Session Discoveries

### Session 5

- [[Eileen Whitebeak]] revealed that [[Corso]] is obsessed with an ancient [[The Senin|Senin]] prophecy that led to the genocide of his people. She refuses to follow that path
