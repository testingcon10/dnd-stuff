# Mana Virus

A virus that afflicted people approximately 150 years ago. A [[The Senin|Senin]] boy named Alex was rumored to be a bringer of the virus. Alex and the [[The Three Sisters|Three Sisters]] sought to find a cure.

[[Father Ellen]], who lost his daughter to the virus, became a prophet and miracle worker who could cure those afflicted. This led to the founding of the [[Church of the Daughter]].
