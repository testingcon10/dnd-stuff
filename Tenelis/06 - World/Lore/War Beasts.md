# War Beasts

Chemically and magically mutated creatures created through alchemical experimentation. Used as weapons of war by the orc warlord [[Vien]] in his campaign to conquer [[Vesin]] and [[Velik]], a few years before the first demon incursion.

## Origins

[[Vien]] developed the war beasts as a weapon for his conquest. The mutation process combines chemical and magical techniques - similar in concept to alchemical mutation experiments.

## Current Status

At least one war beast has survived to the present day. It is being used as the main event in [[Uzog]]'s underground gladiator arena in [[Drayik]].
