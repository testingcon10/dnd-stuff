## Description

A tavern in [[Drayik]] connected to [[The Golds]]' gambling operations. Listed as a lead on the [[Gambling Operations Manifest]] recovered from [[Kay'Dara]]'s lockbox. [[Watkins]] at [[The Half Mast]] directed the party here for the winner-takes-all card game.

The entrance has armed guards who confiscate weapons before entry.
