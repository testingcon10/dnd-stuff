## Description

After the recent attack on [[Delmind]] during the Festival of the Daughter, the church has employed the party to uncover the secrets of the Senin and their plans. [[Eileen Whitebeak]] met with the party and asked for help discovering why recent food shipments have stopped.

## Objectives

- [x] Meet with [[Eileen Whitebeak]] and learn about the Senin
