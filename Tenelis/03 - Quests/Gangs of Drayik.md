## Description

The party has been disrupting criminal operations across [[Drayik]]. After killing [[Kay'Dara]] and recovering the [[Gambling Operations Manifest]], they've identified four factions operating in the city: [[The Golds]], [[Avian Brotherhood]], [[The Feints]], and [[Elven Mafia]]. The [[Kenku]] in the [[Catacombs]] are arming all of them.

## Objectives

- [x] Discover [[The Golds]]' operations at the [[Boar Tusk Brewery]]
- [x] Kill [[Kay'Dara]] and recover intel
- [x] Identify the four criminal factions in [[Drayik]]
- [x] Investigate the [[Gambling Operations Manifest]] leads
- [x] Attend the winner-takes-all card game at [[The Drunken Pig]] ([[Watkins]] at [[The Half Mast]] directed the party there)
- [x] Find [[Uzog]] - leader of [[The Golds]] (home is an underground gladiator arena)
