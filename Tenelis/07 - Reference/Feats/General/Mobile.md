# Mobile

*General Feat (Prerequisite: None)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Speed Increase.** Your Speed increases by 10 feet.

**Dash Over Difficult Terrain.** When you use the Dash action, difficult terrain doesn't cost you extra movement on that turn.

**Agile Striker.** When you make a melee attack against a creature, you don't provoke opportunity attacks from that creature for the rest of the turn, whether you hit or not.
