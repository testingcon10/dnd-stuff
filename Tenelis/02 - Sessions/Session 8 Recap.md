# Session 8

**Date:** 4/7/2026
**Players Present:** [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly]], [[Booker Locke]], [[Old Shell]], [[Cassius Bellona]]

## Previously...

The party infiltrated [[Uzog]]'s underground gladiator arena, entering through the servant quarters where they overheard nobles discussing [[Delmind]] trade disruptions. They learned the night's fight card from the nobles: [[Vien]]'s [[War Beasts|war beast]] as the main event, [[The Golds]] forcing a captured [[The Feints]] member to fight a catacomb carrion crawler, and [[Uzog]]'s traitors fighting each other. [[Booker Locke|Booker]] spotted [[Von]] leaving the compound that morning. After [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] cleaned house at the card game in [[The Drunken Pig]] - winning 3,918 gold and using some of it to upgrade [[Booker Locke|Booker]]'s shortbow to +1 - the party entered the arena ready to watch the fights.

## Summary

The party arrived at the arena, placed bets on the fight card, then killed [[Uzog]] before any of the scheduled fights took place. [[Old Shell]] shot him in the head with a bow. The party ransacked the arena, recovering a dagger ([[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]]) and a greataxe ([[Cassius Bellona|Cassius]]), and got their bet money back. The bartender from [[The Drunken Pig]] is still somewhere in the arena complex.
