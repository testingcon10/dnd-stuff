# Session 11

**Date:** 6/2/2026
**Players Present:**

## Previously...

The party met with [[Eileen Whitebeak]], presented [[Uzog]]'s head as proof of the kill, and received documents on [[Gwen Locke|Gwen]]'s abduction trail - her father worked with the [[Avian Brotherhood]], and the [[Church of the Daughter|Church]] intercepted the abducted girls before [[Gwen Locke|Gwen]] went fully missing. In exchange for a talisman, Eileen sent her lieutenant [[August]] along for the mission. During the week-long trek to [[Little Hill]], the party bonded with [[August]] - [[Cassius Bellona|Cassius]] spoke his language and he opened up about his hard upbringing and his changed view of Eileen's brother. Arriving at [[Little Hill]], they found the town nearly deserted. Three girls had gone missing in sequence - [[Aggie]] ([[Dimitri]]'s daughter), [[Katrina]], and [[Lana]] - each disappearance driving more residents away. [[Dimitri]] had grown erratic before vanishing entirely. The only lead: [[Masha]], an old woman north of town, last known to have seen him.

### Lore
1. [[Masha]] is the last member of an unnamed order dedicated to preventing the return of this threat
2. 100 years ago, girls began going missing in a town similar to [[Little Hill]] - the disappearances correlated with stars going out, and the pattern grew
3. [[Masha]] believes [[The Three Sisters]] and [[The Senin]] were supposed to stop this threat but may not have succeeded
4. [[Masha]] believes a demon has been occupying the mountains near [[Little Hill]] for hundreds of years
5. Approximately 200 years ago, a great demon war was waged - led by the forces of Grazid, an archdemon
6. Conspirators helped Grazid's forces break the barrier between this reality and other planes, allowing a full demonic invasion
7. [[Knights of Drayik|The Knights of Drayik]] were one of the leading forces in securing victory for this world during the demon war - at great cost, with many knights dying
8. It was believed after the war that all demons had been vanquished or had retreated - the running theory is that at least one demon survived and has been living in the mountains ever since
9. The demon in the mountains is the party's leading theory for what has been taking the girls
10. The scarecrow attack on [[Masha]]'s house suggests the mountain entity is aware of her - and potentially aware of the party now too
11. The kidnappings are not isolated to [[Little Hill]] - a nearby town has also experienced disappearances
12. [[Masha]] believes the [[Mana Virus]] and the kidnappings are connected somehow
13. [[Sophie]] (one of [[The Three Sisters]]) was the first to notice the kidnappings during a previous cycle - by then, 3 girls were already missing, possibly more
