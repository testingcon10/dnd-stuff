# Session 9

**Date:** 4/14/2026
**Players Present:**

## Previously...

The party infiltrated [[Uzog]]'s underground gladiator arena and placed bets on the fight card. Before any of the fights took place, [[Old Shell]] shot [[Uzog]] in the head with a bow. The party ransacked the arena - [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] took a dagger, [[Cassius Bellona|Cassius]] took a greataxe, and everyone got their bet money back. The bartender from [[The Drunken Pig]] was still somewhere in the arena complex. The unnamed dock owner who lost at cards was supposed to meet [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] at the arena.

## Summary

After ransacking [[Uzog]]'s arena, the party found 2 orc guards outside and coerced them into leading the group down into the fight pits. There they found the [[Elven Mafia]] thug from the fight card - a man named [[Quinn]]. They robbed the orc guards for 8 gold and interrogated [[Quinn]], extracting a large amount of intel on the syndicate operations in [[Drayik]]: [[Von]] controls the docks and all shipping, runs human trafficking, drugs, and weapons. The gangs meet with [[The Senin]] regarding [[Catacombs|the catacombs]], which are critical to the human trafficking pipeline. [[The Feints]] supply a large influx of magical weapons, some of which end up in [[Catacombs|the catacombs]]. The [[Elven Mafia]] operates out of a shipyard on the outskirts of [[Drayik]], near the shore.

## Key Events

### Story
1. Party found 2 orc guards outside the arena and coerced/threatened them into leading the party into the fight pits
2. Party robbed the 2 orc guards for 8 gold
3. Party found [[Quinn]] - the [[Elven Mafia]] thug from the arena fight card - at the entrance to the fight pits
4. Party interrogated [[Quinn]], who gave up extensive intel on [[Drayik]] syndicate operations
5. Party found the bartender from [[The Drunken Pig]] - his name is [[Palmer]]. Party let him go
6. Party found [[Ross]] - the [[The Golds|Gold]] thug from fight card 1 - actually a spy for [[The Feints]]
7. [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] proposed a dagger trade to test whether [[Ross]] had a magical weapon from [[The Feints]]; [[Ross]] claimed he did not, then pickpocketed [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]]'s dagger and took it back - his weapon is attuned to him
8. Party interrogated and began recruiting [[Ross]] - offered him house, coin, food, and glory
9. Party laid out their goal: disrupt the supply lines to [[Catacombs|the catacombs]] and end the current state of corruption
10. [[Ross]] said the party is too small to take on [[The Feints]] currently - but perked up when the party mentioned larger allies ([[Eileen Whitebeak]], the church, etc.)
11. [[Ross]] began opening up about [[The Feints]]' information network and operations
12. Party opened [[Ross]]'s cell - a [[War Beasts|Vien's war beast]] was released with him
13. Party and [[Ross]] fought and killed the [[War Beasts|war beast]] together
14. Party left [[Uzog]]'s arena
15. Party sold some items
16. Party went to [[Red Elk]] for dinner

### Lore
1. Arena attendees include nobles, mafia members, and church members
2. [[Von]] runs the docks and all shipping in [[Drayik]] - controls human trafficking, drugs, and weapons
3. The gangs meet with [[The Senin]] regarding [[Catacombs|the catacombs]] - [[Catacombs|the catacombs]] are central to the smuggling network, specifically human trafficking
4. [[The Feints]] supply a large influx of magical weapons into [[Drayik]] - some end up in [[Catacombs|the catacombs]]
5. The [[Elven Mafia]] operates out of a shipyard on the outskirts of [[Drayik]], near the shore
6. [[The Feints]] operate at a much larger scale than just gambling rings - their scope is far more villainous
7. [[Ross]] ([[The Feints|Feints]] spy) believes [[Corso]]'s plans, if achieved, would be a glorious cause
8. [[Ross]] views the militia ([[The Senin|the Senin]] liberation arm) as having horse blinders - rigid point A to B thinking with no nuance. Thinks things might be changing but it's been a long time since any real change
9. [[Ross]]'s dagger is attuned to him - suggests it is magical
10. Getting information on [[The Feints]] is extremely difficult - [[Ross]] has never met the man who runs them
11. [[The Feints]] use a network of info runners who report to a stone golem in a warehouse; where the information goes after that is unknown
12. [[The Feints]] operate a shipping warehouse as a front - contains a large sentry and is where info runners relay information. This is a separate location from the [[Elven Mafia]]'s shipyard
13. Party's next goal: learn more about the info runners; use [[Ross]] to potentially flip some of them
