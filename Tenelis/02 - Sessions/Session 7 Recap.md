# Session 7

**Date:** 3/31/2026
**Players Present:**

## Previously...

The party followed leads from the [[Gambling Operations Manifest]] recovered from [[Kay'Dara]]'s lockbox. At [[The Half Mast]], they coerced the bartender [[Watkins]] into revealing a winner-takes-all card game happening at [[The Drunken Pig]] - a [[The Golds|Golds]]-run gambling operation where [[Uzog]] himself might show up. Meanwhile, the party visited [[Old Drayik Keep]], where [[Payton Hightower]] granted library access. They uncovered intel on underground river networks, [[Kenku]] family lines, the [[Mana Virus]] history, and [[The Three Sisters]]. [[Booker Locke|Booker]] infiltrated the forbidden section disguised as a priest but was caught, forcing the party to leave.

## Summary

[[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] purchased a [[Basic Poison|basic poison]] recipe from [[Tara]], and the party crafted poisons before heading to [[The Drunken Pig]] - the [[The Golds|Golds]]-connected tavern that [[Watkins]] at [[The Half Mast]] had pointed them to from the [[Gambling Operations Manifest]] leads. Guards confiscated [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]]'s weapons at the door. [[Booker Locke|Booker]], working the room in disguise, learned that [[Uzog]] himself would make an appearance if the night went long enough. [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] sat down at the card table with a 300 GP buyin, won 900 gold on his first hand, and proceeded to clean house - walking away with 3,918 gold total. At the table, he chatted up a losing player who refused to give his name but revealed he works in shipping and owns docks in [[Drayik]], promising to meet [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] at the arena. [[Booker Locke|Booker]], [[Cassius Bellona|Cassius]], and [[Old Shell]] held their positions through the night. Afterward, the party shared backstories for the first time - [[Cassius Bellona|Cassius]] told the story of the [[Knights of Drayik]], and [[Old Shell]] shared war stories. [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] then opened the envelope from the [[Gambling Operations Manifest]] - it contained [[Uzog]]'s home address. The party already had this info, but the real revelation was that [[Uzog]]'s home doubles as an underground gladiator arena, and the party was now invited. [[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] spent 1,000 gold of his winnings upgrading [[Booker Locke|Booker]]'s shortbow to +1 at the armorer. The next morning, the party scouted [[Uzog]]'s compound - built into a mound with no windows. [[Booker Locke|Booker]] spotted [[Von]] leaving in his carriage. The party entered through the servant quarters, where nobles were discussing [[Delmind]] trade disruptions. They learned the arena's main card was [[Vien]]'s [[War Beasts|war beast]] - a relic from the orc warlord's campaign to conquer [[Vesin]] and [[Velik]] before the first demon incursion. [[Booker Locke|Booker]] asked the nobles about the undercard: [[The Golds]] had captured one of [[The Feints]] to fight a catacomb carrion crawler, and [[Uzog]] was forcing his own traitors to fight each other. [[Von]] tried hyping the party's recent fights in [[Drayik]] to [[Uzog]], who brushed it off. The party entered the arena.
