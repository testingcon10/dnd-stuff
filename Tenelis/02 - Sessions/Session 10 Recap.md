# Session 10

**Date:** 5/19/2026
**Players Present:**

## Previously...

After ransacking [[Uzog]]'s arena, the party coerced orc guards into leading them to the fight pits, interrogated [[Quinn]] ([[Elven Mafia]]) for intel on [[Drayik]]'s syndicate operations, freed and began recruiting [[Ross]] (a [[The Feints|Feints]] spy posing as a Gold thug), and fought a [[War Beasts|war beast]] alongside him. The party learned that Von controls the docks and all shipping, [[The Feints|the Feints]] supply magical weapons via a stone golem info network, and [[Catacombs|the catacombs]] are central to the smuggling pipeline. The party left the arena and had dinner at [[Red Elk]].

## Summary

[[Netanyahu D. Kirkuenly|Net]] visited [[Aya]] to learn about poison mastery, receiving [[Drow Poison|drow poison]] and a crafting recipe, and paid 200 gold for help crafting his first batch of potions. The party then went to [[Mana House|the Mana House]] to see [[Grong]] and [[Umble]], where [[Old Shell]] bought a talisman and learned the ingredients needed to craft his own. The party rested through the day before heading out to meet [[Eileen Whitebeak]] for the follow-up they had agreed upon.

10. [[Dimitri]] has existing relationships with local farmers and negotiates deals to work around the collapsed supply lines

12. [[Little Hill]] is a ghost town - nearly all residents have fled or vanished
13. 3 girls have gone missing from [[Little Hill]]: [[Aggie]] ([[Dimitri]]'s daughter), [[Katrina]], and [[Lana]] - disappearances happened in sequence, each causing greater panic than the last
14. [[Dimitri]] became increasingly disheveled and erratic before he disappeared
15. [[Masha]] (old woman, lives north of town) was the last known person to see [[Dimitri]] - bartender considers her a lead; blacksmith considers her crazy
