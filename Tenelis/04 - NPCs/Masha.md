## Appearance

Old woman. Described as scraggly.

## Personality

Considered eccentric or crazy by the [[Little Hill]] blacksmith. The bartender treats her as a credible lead. Guarded at first, but opened up once the party claimed to be friends of [[Dimitri]]. Carries the weight of ancient duty - grim and matter-of-fact about the threat.

## Background

Lives north of [[Little Hill]]. Was the last known person to see [[Dimitri]] before he disappeared. Witnessed his deterioration firsthand.

Last surviving member of an unnamed ancient order dedicated to preventing the return of a demonic threat tied to the mountains. Her order has been watching for the signs - girls going missing, stars going out - for generations. She had hoped [[The Three Sisters]] and [[The Senin]] had ended the threat for good, but the events at [[Little Hill]] have shaken that hope.

## The Order

[[Masha]] is the last of her order. Their purpose: ensure that the demon that survived the great demon war does not resurge. The order has kept watch over the pattern - whenever a star goes out, girls begin to go missing. This happened 100 years ago in a town similar to [[Little Hill]].
